# Gnimu Monitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Gnimu Monitor is an app for viewing live telemetry from a Gnimu GNSS+IMU device over Bluetooth Low Energy. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. The developer does not collect, receive, store or share any data from your use of the app. Gnimu Monitor contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and does not make its own network requests.

## Data the app handles on your device

- **Telemetry from your Gnimu device.** While connected, the app reads position, speed, motion, time and battery data from the device over Bluetooth and shows it on screen. The position is the Gnimu device's, not your phone's; the app does not request access to your location.
- **Captures and reports.** If you record a capture, the telemetry is saved as a file, and an analysis report is saved next to it. Captures are stored in your iCloud Drive (in a "Gnimu Monitor" folder) if iCloud is available, otherwise in the app's storage on your device, where you can see them in the Files app. They are not sent to the developer. If you store them in iCloud, Apple handles them under your iCloud terms. They leave your device elsewhere only if you choose to share them.
- **Settings.** The app remembers preferences such as the speed unit on your device.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find and connect to your Gnimu device. It is not used to identify you or to collect data about other devices.

## Maps

The map in the app is provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). Showing map tiles involves requests to Apple's servers, which Apple handles under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer does not receive that information.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
